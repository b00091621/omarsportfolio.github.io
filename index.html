Automated BFRB Detection System

Senior project: a non-intrusive, camera-based system that automatically detects and classifies body-focused repetitive behaviors such as nail biting and hair pulling from ordinary RGB video, using pose landmark features and deep learning for real-time behavioral awareness without wearable devices.

PythonMATLABOpenCVMediaPipe Holistic
A privacy-conscious, camera-based framework for the automatic detection and classification of Body-Focused Repetitive Behaviors (BFRBs) — recurrent, self-directed actions such as nail biting, hair pulling, cuticle picking, and knuckle cracking that can cause physical harm and emotional distress when left unmonitored. Unlike approaches relying on wearable sensors or controlled clinical observation, the system operates entirely from ordinary RGB video captured by four Logitech Brio 500 cameras positioned at front, left, right, and top viewpoints, recording 20 participants performing 24 distinct behavior classes — 11 BFRBs and 13 normal daily activities — nine times per camera view, yielding 720 sequences per class. Raw frames are processed with MediaPipe Holistic to extract 75 skeletal landmarks per frame, from which a 275-dimensional feature vector is computed, combining raw 3D landmark coordinates with engineered distance and joint-angle features designed to capture hand-to-face proximity and repetitive motion patterns. Each sequence is resampled to a fixed 25-frame window (a 5-second observation period), producing 25×275 input tensors passed to five architectures for temporal classification: a stacked LSTM baseline, a Temporal Convolutional Network using dilated causal convolutions, a LightweightMV2 model with depthwise separable 1D convolutions, a Vanilla Transformer with multi-head self-attention, and a CNN-Transformer hybrid. All models are evaluated under Leave-One-Subject-Out cross-validation, testing exclusively on unseen participants to reflect real-world generalization — aiming to enable timely, non-intrusive behavioral awareness on consumer-grade hardware without wearables or cloud processing.
